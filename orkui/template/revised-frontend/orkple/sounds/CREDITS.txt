ORKPLE II sound credits
=======================

disk-read.m4a
-------------
Plays under the ORKEK title card when a game loads, and under the ORKPLE ][
banner during the power-on boot (see diskSound() in ../orkple-terminal.js).

What is known:
  Original title : "reading floppy disc 2"
  From           : the "freesound community" (described as royalty free)
  Supplied by    : the project maintainer
  Edits          : trimmed to the first 4 seconds, normalized, 250ms fade-out.
                   The edited clip is a derivative work of the original.
